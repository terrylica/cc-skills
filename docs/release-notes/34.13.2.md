## [34.13.2](https://github.com/terrylica/cc-skills/compare/v34.13.1...v34.13.2) (2026-11-11)

A note parked in Apple Notes for the operator's review ended "15:02 MST" on 2026-11-11. British Columbia has kept UTC−7 all year since 2026-11-01 and names that time Pacific time, PCT; macOS's zone database labels the same offset "MST", and the draft-park footer printed the system's label as it was. The Telegram footer and the status line were corrected in v34.13.0, and this footer was missed. This release corrects the label in the one remaining place, so a parked draft no longer shows a zone name the operator has ruled out, and no caller needs the `TZ=PCT7` workaround.

### Summary

- **draft-park footer:** a stamp taken in `America/Vancouver` or `Canada/Pacific`, on or after 2026-11-01 09:00 UTC, at UTC−7, is labelled PCT. The wall clock still comes from the system's `date`; only the label changes.
- **Pure functions:** `zoneLabel()` decides the label from the system's label, the offset, the zone and the instant, and `stampFrom()` builds the footer's stamp from `date "+%Y-%m-%d %H:%M %Z %z"`. `nowStamp()` calls them, so the rule is testable without a clock.
- **A stale host stays visible:** a machine whose zone data predates the change reports PST, an hour off, and that label is left as it is, so the wrong clock is not hidden behind a correct-looking name.
- **Other zones untouched:** Phoenix and Edmonton keep "MST", and malformed `date` output is passed through.
- **Tests:** five unit tests, including the Mac's real "MST -0700" output and pre-change PDT and PST stamps. `repo:lint` and `repo:test` pass (3,089 tests).
- **Skill record:** the draft-park `SKILL.md` evolution log names the change and its cause.
